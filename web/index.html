How a call flows

  1. Your microphonestreamed as 8 kHz audio, the same format a phone call uses
  2. Deepgram Nova-3live speech-to-text, English and Hindi in one stream
  3. Llama 3.3 70B on Groqwrites the reply, starting with a reaction tag
  4. A recorded reaction plays at once"hmm", "achha": you hear Myra before the full reply is ready
  5. Sarvam AI voicespeaks the reply sentence by sentence

Try this

  • Say hello first; Myra waits for you, like on a real call.
  • Switch to Hindi: प्लॉट का प्राइस क्या है?
  • Talk over her while she's speaking. She stops and listens.
  • Call again later from this browser. She remembers what you discussed.

Calls last up to 3 minutes. Audio isn't stored; Myra keeps a short summary of your call so she can pick up where you left off.
